Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Focus Town ("we", "us") is a mobile focus-timer game operated by [DEVELOPER NAME]. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

1. Information stored on your device only

Your focus sessions (start time, length, mode, result), streaks, town, and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. You can erase them anytime in Settings → Reset all records or by uninstalling the app.

2. Information we collect (optional)

If "Share crash & usage data" is on (it is on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Privacy), we collect:

  • Anonymous usage events: app opened, onboarding finished or skipped, focus session started / cancelled / completed / failed, with session length, mode category (custom mode names are never sent), selected background music, and reason for ending.
  • Technical data: platform (iOS/Android), app version, and a random anonymous ID generated on your device.
  • Crash reports: error details, device model, and OS version when the app crashes.

If you create an account (optional; needed for group sessions and keeping your character on other devices), we also store your email address, your display name, and your character (animal, color, hat). We use your email only to sign you in with a one-time code, and your name and character are shown to people in your group sessions. We also store who you follow and who follows you, and your friend code, so you can add friends and invite them. For group sessions we also store the room you joined, when you joined and left, and how long you were away from the app during the session, which is visible to the other members of that room. You can delete your account anytime in Settings → Account → Delete account, which permanently removes this data.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone/camera data, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising, and you can use the solo mode without an account.

3. How we use it

Only to fix bugs, understand how the app is used (for example, how many sessions are completed), and improve it. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

4. Service providers

ProviderPurpose
PostHogAnonymous usage analytics
Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.)Crash reporting
SupabaseSign-in and storage of account/profile data (only if you create an account)

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it in the United States or the European Union.

5. Notifications

The app uses local notifications on your device to warn you when you leave the app during a session and, if you turn it on, to send a daily reminder at the time you choose. These are scheduled on your device.

If you have an account and allow notifications, the app also stores a push-notification token for your device so that a teammate in a group session can send you a "come back" nudge when you have left the app. The token is delivered through Expo's push-notification service and is removed when you sign out or delete your account. Other users cannot see it.

6. Retention

Analytics and crash data are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Account and profile data are kept until you delete your account. Data on your device stays until you delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off data sharing anytime in Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete on-device data via Settings → Reset all records or by uninstalling.
  • Because analytics use a random ID that is not linked to your identity, we may not be able to locate your data; you can still contact us with questions or requests at [CONTACT EMAIL].

8. Children

Focus Town is not directed to children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes

If we add features that handle more data (such as group session history), we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent. The effective date above shows the latest version.

이름, 이메일, 전화번호, 연락처, 위치, 사진, 마이크/카메라 정보, 광고 식별자는 수집하지 않습니다. 앱에는 광고가 없으며, 혼자 하기 모드는 회원가입 없이 사용할 수 있습니다.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Focus Town ("we", "us") is a mobile focus-timer game operated by [DEVELOPER NAME]. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have.

1. Information stored on your device only

Your focus sessions (start time, length, mode, result), streaks, town, and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. You can erase them anytime in Settings → Reset all records or by uninstalling the app.

2. Information we collect (optional)

If "Share crash & usage data" is on (it is on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Privacy), we collect:

  • Anonymous usage events: app opened, onboarding finished or skipped, focus session started / cancelled / completed / failed, with session length, mode category (custom mode names are never sent), selected background music, and reason for ending.
  • Technical data: platform (iOS/Android), app version, and a random anonymous ID generated on your device.
  • Crash reports: error details, device model, and OS version when the app crashes.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone/camera data, or advertising identifiers. The app has no account or advertising in this version.

3. How we use it

Only to fix bugs, understand how the app is used (for example, how many sessions are completed), and improve it. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

4. Service providers

ProviderPurpose
PostHogAnonymous usage analytics
Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.)Crash reporting

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it in the United States or the European Union.

5. Notifications

The app uses local notifications on your device to warn you when you leave the app during a session. No push-notification token is collected.

6. Retention

Analytics and crash data are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Data on your device stays until you delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off data sharing anytime in Settings → Privacy.
  • Delete on-device data via Settings → Reset all records or by uninstalling.
  • Because analytics use a random ID that is not linked to your identity, we may not be able to locate your data; you can still contact us with questions or requests at [CONTACT EMAIL].

8. Children

Focus Town is not directed to children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes

If we add features that handle more data (such as accounts or group sessions), we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent. The effective date above shows the latest version.
